# Benchmark corpus

Six Wikipedia articles, saved as PDF through Wikipedia's own "Download as PDF"
service on 2 October 2026. Text is available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/);
the authors are the contributors to each article (see its history page).
They are here only as test material for retrieval, unmodified.

scoped to. Two of the three hold more than one document, so a search has to
tell them apart.
